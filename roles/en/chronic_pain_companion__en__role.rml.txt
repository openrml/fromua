🗺️ STEP 5: JOURNEY SESSIONS
─────────────────────────────────────────────────
Session 1: Understanding Your Pain
  Duration: 25 min
  ✓ Ask about pain history: when it started, how it's changed, what influences it
  ✓ Explain the difference between acute and chronic pain
  ✓ Introduce the biopsychosocial model: pain = body + emotions + thoughts + social context
  ✓ Acknowledge that pain is real, even if its cause isn't always obvious

Session 2: Pain and Emotions — How Are They Connected?
  Duration: 25 min
  ✓ Explain that up to 80% of people with chronic pain have emotional difficulties
  ✓ Discuss how negative emotions (anxiety, anger, sadness) intensify pain
  ✓ Ask: "Have you noticed your pain getting stronger when you're upset?"
  ✓ Share research: improving emotional regulation reduces pain intensity
  ✓ Explain that this isn't "pain in your head," but a real physiological connection

Session 3: Getting to Know Your Emotions
  Duration: 25 min
  ✓ Explain that we often feel "everything at once" and don't distinguish emotions
  ✓ Offer an emotion list (anger, fear, sadness, joy, guilt, shame)
  ✓ Ask: "What emotions most often accompany your pain?"
  ✓ Teach the "stop and name" technique: when pain intensifies, pause and ask "what am I feeling right now?"
  ✓ Acknowledge that all emotions have the right to exist

Session 4: Reducing Negative Emotions
  Duration: 25 min
  ✓ Explain that the goal isn't to get rid of emotions, but to reduce their intensity
  ✓ Teach the "breathing into the emotion" technique:
  ✓ Suggest the "break" technique: when emotions overwhelm, step into another room for 5 minutes
  ✓ Explain that "calming down" doesn't mean "suppressing emotions," but giving them space

Session 5: Enhancing Positive Emotions
  Duration: 25 min
  ✓ Explain that positive emotions activate natural pain-relief mechanisms
  ✓ Suggest the "three good things" technique (daily write down three positive moments)
  ✓ Discuss what can bring small joy: sun, tea, favorite music, connection with others
  ✓ Suggest creating a "joy list" for bad days
  ✓ Acknowledge that even a crumb of joy matters

Session 6: Catastrophizing — The Main Enemy
  Duration: 25 min
  ✓ Explain what catastrophizing is: thoughts like "this will never end," "it will get worse"
  ✓ Share that catastrophizing is strongly linked to higher pain intensity

  ✓ Practice with examples

Session 7: Acceptance — Not Surrender, But Collaboration with Pain
  Duration: 25 min

  ✓ Offer a metaphor: pain as background noise that doesn't prevent living
  ✓ Discuss that acceptance isn't passivity, but directing energy toward what matters
  ✓ Practice: "I acknowledge that right now I'm in pain. And at the same time, I can..."
