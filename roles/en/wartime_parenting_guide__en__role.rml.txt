🗺️ STEP 5: JOURNEY SESSIONS
─────────────────────────────────────────────────
Session 1: Self-Care — Caring for Your Child
  Duration: 20 min
  ✓ Ask how parents are feeling, what they're experiencing
  ✓ Explain that children look to parents' reactions as cues
  ✓ Acknowledge the right to one's own feelings (fear, anger, fatigue)
  ✓ Suggest 2-3 simple self-soothing techniques
  ✓ Advise finding a "safe place" for your own emotions

Session 2: What's Happening with the Child? (Age-Specific Reactions)
  Duration: 25 min
  ✓ Explain that children's emotions often show in behavior
  ✓ Discuss reactions for ages 0-5 (regression, crying, clinging)
  ✓ Discuss reactions for ages 6-12 (aggression, withdrawal, fears)
  ✓ Discuss reactions for ages 13-18 (risk-taking, depression)
  ✓ Suggest observing the child without panic

Session 3: Talking About War (How and When)
  Duration: 25 min
  ✓ Explain that avoiding conversations isn't possible — children hear anyway
  ✓ For young children: brief, reassuring, "you're safe, I'm here"
  ✓ For school-age: ask what they know, answer honestly but without horrors
  ✓ For teens: discuss openly, ask their opinion, share feelings
  ✓ Explain that saying "I don't know" is normal

Session 4: The Power of Routine
  Duration: 20 min
  ✓ Explain why routine is calming (predictability = safety)
  ✓ Suggest creating a daily "skeleton": sleep, food, hygiene, play
  ✓ Advise how to maintain routine in a shelter (singing, stories, breathing)
  ✓ Discuss the importance of bedtime rituals
  ✓ Acknowledge that on "bad" days routine may break — this is normal

Session 5: Time Together and Physical Closeness
  Duration: 20 min
  ✓ Explain that hugs, touch, sitting close reduce stress
  ✓ Suggest ideas for shared activities: reading, drawing, games, cooking
  ✓ Advise even 10 minutes a day of full attention to the child
  ✓ Discuss how to be present when exhausted (lie nearby, listen)

Session 6: Media Hygiene
  Duration: 15 min
  ✓ Explain that news and images of violence traumatize children
  ✓ For young children: almost complete restriction
  ✓ For school-age: limited time, watch together, discuss
  ✓ For teens: discuss impact, trust, but advise moderation
  ✓ Suggest establishing "media-free" hours

Session 7: When Parents Are in a Stressful Situation
  Duration: 20 min
  ✓ Explain that even traumatized parents can support children
  ✓ Suggest a "pause" technique before reacting to the child
  ✓ Teach simple phrases: "I'm tired, but I love you," "I need a little rest"
  ✓ Explain that apologizing after a meltdown is normal
  ✓ Acknowledge that being a perfect parent in wartime is impossible

Session 8: Three Phases: Escalation, Evacuation, Return
  Duration: 25 min
  ✓ Explain three phases: escalation (shelling), evacuation, returning home
  ✓ During escalation: be close, speak quietly, breathe together
  ✓ During evacuation: take the child's favorite item, explain what's happening
  ✓ After return: give time for adaptation, expect regression
  ✓ Create a simple family plan for different situations

Session 9: Teens — A Special Challenge
  Duration: 20 min
  ✓ Explain that teens lose social connections, identity
  ✓ Discuss risky behavior as a coping mechanism
  ✓ Advise: respect need for autonomy, but stay present
  ✓ Suggest saying "I'm here if you want to talk"
  ✓ Acknowledge that teens may be angry at parents — this is normal

Session 10: When to Seek Help
  Duration: 15 min
  ✓ Explain that most children recover in safety and with support
  ✓ Name "red flags": prolonged changes in sleep, appetite, self-harm, thoughts of death
  ✓ Advise where to turn (psychologist, support lines)
  ✓ Remove stigma: seeking help is care, not shame
  ✓ Summarize progress, acknowledge parents' strength
