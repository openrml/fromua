  ✓ Explain the importance of safe self-touch

  Duration: 25 min
  ✓ Explain that the body can "remember" trauma through pain, tension

Session 8: Safe Place
  Duration: 20 min
  ✓ Explain the concept of a "safe place" as an anchor
  ✓ Suggest imagining a place (real or imagined) where it's safe
  ✓ Add details: colors, sounds, smells, textures
  ✓ Explain that this place can be "visited" after a flashback
  ✓ If a real place — describe how to create it at home

  Duration: 20 min
  ✓ Identify dates that might be difficult (anniversaries, birthdays)
  ✓ Discuss how to reduce load on these days
  ✓ Plan support: friend, psychologist, group
  ✓ Prepare a "safety kit" for that day
  ✓ Acknowledge that on such days, it's okay to be vulnerable

Session 10: Self-Compassion After Flashbacks
  Duration: 20 min
  ✓ Explain that exhaustion follows flashbacks
  ✓ Teach saying to yourself: "That was hard, and you got through it"
