🗺️ STEP 5: JOURNEY SESSIONS
─────────────────────────────────────────────────
Session 1: Stop and Acknowledge
  Duration: 25 min
  ✓ Ask what happened and what the person is feeling now
  ✓ Explain that denial, anger, blame are normal, but they hinder action
  ✓ Help navigate the journey: denial → anger → bargaining → acceptance
  ✓ Acknowledge: "you have the right to these feelings, but from here we'll look forward"

Session 2: Audit — What Do You Have?
  Duration: 30 min
  ✓ Professional skills: education, experience, certificates
  ✓ "Hidden" skills: volunteering, hobbies, household abilities
  ✓ Social connections: who can help, advise, support
  ✓ Physical assets: what can be sold, rented, used

Session 3: Quick Formats — What Can Be Done Right Now?
  Duration: 25 min
  ✓ Discuss temporary work options (delivery, coffee shops, assembly)
  ✓ Freelance platforms: what skills can be monetized online
  ✓ Unexpected opportunities: generators, paid volunteering
  ✓ Important: any income now is a step, even if not "prestigious"

Session 4: Government Support — What's Available?
  Duration: 25 min
  ✓ Micro-grants up to 42,000 UAH for IDPs, veterans, affected persons
  ✓ Retraining and internship programs
  ✓ Aid from international organizations (Red Cross, etc.)
  ✓ How to apply, what documents are needed

Session 5: Crisis Budget
  Duration: 25 min
  ✓ Identify minimum essential expenses (food, housing, medicine, transport)
  ✓ Determine what can be temporarily eliminated
  ✓ Allocate available and expected funds
  ✓ Explain the importance of tracking every hryvnia

Session 6: Debt — Don't Hide, Communicate
  Duration: 20 min
  ✓ Explain: ignoring debts worsens the situation
  ✓ How to talk to banks: restructuring, credit holidays
  ✓ Warn against microloans with huge interest
  ✓ Priority: basic needs first, then debts

Session 7: Networking — How to Find Work Through People
  Duration: 20 min
  ✓ Explain that most vacancies are filled through acquaintances
  ✓ Create a list of people to inform about your search
  ✓ How to write messages without being intrusive
  ✓ Career consultants, headhunters as a resource

Session 8: Short-Term Strategy (3 Months)
  Duration: 25 min
  ✓ Choose 1-2 directions for development from skills audit
  ✓ Identify what's missing (training, certification, connections)
  ✓ Form a list of actions for each month
  ✓ Set realistic income goals

Session 9: Diversification — Don't Put All Eggs in One Basket
  Duration: 20 min
  ✓ Explain the importance of diversification in crisis times
  ✓ Explore: what other products/services could be offered
  ✓ Parallel skills: what else can be done simultaneously
  ✓ Passive income: is it possible in current conditions

Session 10: Emergency Fund — Safety Cushion
  Duration: 20 min
  ✓ Explain that 3-6 months of expenses saved means security
  ✓ How to start: even 50-100 UAH per week
  ✓ Automatic transfers to a separate account
  ✓ Even a small fund reduces anxiety

Session 11: Long-Term Strategy (1 Year+)
  Duration: 25 min
  ✓ Return to skills audit: which directions have potential
  ✓ Education and development: what's worth learning in a year
  ✓ Possibility of own business or self-employment
  ✓ Setting financial goals for the year

Session 12: Summary — I'm Not Where I Was
  Duration: 20 min
  ✓ Return to first session — what has changed
  ✓ Choose 3-5 strategies that work best
  ✓ Create a "financial journal" for ongoing tracking
  ✓ Acknowledge that even small steps are progress
