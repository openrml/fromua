🗺️ STEP 5: JOURNEY SESSIONS
─────────────────────────────────────────────────
Session 1: Understanding Your Loneliness
  Duration: 25 min
  ✓ Ask how the person describes their experience of loneliness
  ✓ Explain the difference between social isolation (objective state) and loneliness (subjective feeling)
  ✓ Share that loneliness is a signal, not a life sentence
  ✓ Acknowledge that this is a common issue that shouldn't bring shame

Session 2: Overcoming Negative Thinking
  Duration: 25 min
  ✓ Explain that loneliness is often accompanied by negative thoughts
  ✓ Explore typical beliefs: "I'm not interesting to anyone," "I'll be rejected"
  ✓ Teach the "catch and replace" technique: notice a negative thought and replace it with a more realistic one
  ✓ Explain that people often overestimate the risk of rejection

Session 3: Small Steps — Microconnections
  Duration: 20 min
  ✓ Explain the concept of "microconnections": quick chat with a neighbor, greeting a barista, short message
  ✓ Suggest choosing 1-2 microconnections per day
  ✓ Explain that these small actions gradually reduce anxiety
  ✓ Keep a microconnection journal (what was done, how it felt)

Session 4: Exploring Interests
  Duration: 25 min
  ✓ Create a list of what the person loves to do (or used to love)
  ✓ Explore: hobbies, sports, creativity, books, movies, volunteering
  ✓ For each interest, think about where to find like-minded people
  ✓ Explain that shared interests are a natural foundation for friendship

Session 5: Volunteering as a Path to Community
  Duration: 20 min
  ✓ Explain that volunteering provides not only social contacts but also a sense of meaning and belonging
  ✓ Discuss options: animal shelters, food banks, community organizations
  ✓ Important: choose something genuinely interesting, not what "should" be done
  ✓ Find 1-2 organizations for initial contact

Session 6: Meet-up Groups — Connecting Through Interests
  Duration: 20 min
  ✓ Share about platforms: Meetup.com, Facebook groups, Telegram channels
  ✓ Explore what groups exist in the person's city/country
  ✓ Suggest starting with online meetings if in-person feels intimidating
  ✓ Important: no commitment needed upfront — just come and observe

Session 7: Reconnecting with Old Contacts
  Duration: 25 min
  ✓ Explain that people often want to be reached out to but are afraid to reach out first
  ✓ Create a list of people with whom there were good connections in the past
  ✓ Teach simple phrases to start:
  ✓ Explain that even if they don't respond — it's not a failure

Session 8: Social Anxiety — How to Be in New Places
  Duration: 25 min
  ✓ Explain the "small steps" strategy for people with social anxiety:
  ✓ Gradually increase time spent
  ✓ Practice calming breathing before going out

Session 9: The 50-Hour Rule
  Duration: 20 min
  ✓ Share research: moving from acquaintance to friendship takes about 50 hours of shared time
  ✓ Explain what this means: regular meetings, shared activities
  ✓ Help plan how to create these "hours" (regular clubs, shared projects)
  ✓ Acknowledge that not every acquaintance will become a friendship — and that's normal

Session 10: Online Communities as a Bridge
  Duration: 20 min
  ✓ Explain that online groups can be a safe way to practice social skills
  ✓ Find interest-based groups on social media, Telegram, Discord
  ✓ Start with reading, then commenting, then connecting
  ✓ Discuss how to transition from online to offline meetings if comfortable

Session 11: Quality, Not Quantity
  Duration: 20 min
  ✓ Explain that quality matters more than quantity for happiness
  ✓ Explore what traits are important in friends for this person
  ✓ Teach recognizing "one's own" people
  ✓ Acknowledge that even 1-2 close friends are enough

Session 12: Summary — I'm No Longer Alone
  Duration: 20 min
  ✓ Return to the first session — what has changed in the feeling of loneliness?
  ✓ Choose 2-3 strategies that work best
  ✓ Create a maintenance plan for new connections in the coming months
  ✓ Acknowledge the courage in emerging from isolation
