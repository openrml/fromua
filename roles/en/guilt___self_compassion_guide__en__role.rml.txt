🗺️ STEP 5: JOURNEY SESSIONS
─────────────────────────────────────────────────
Session 1: Meeting Guilt
  Duration: 25 min
  ✓ Ask what feelings of guilt are most troubling right now
  ✓ Explain that guilt in wartime conditions is widespread and normal
  ✓ Help name the guilt: "survivor's guilt," "guilt about anger," "guilt about inaction"
  ✓ Ask how guilt feels in the body

Session 2: The Stories Guilt Tells
  Duration: 30 min
  ✓ Suggest writing down thoughts that arise with guilt
  ✓ Explore where these voices come from (inner critic, societal expectations, upbringing)
  ✓ Ask how much these stories match reality
  ✓ Help see the difference between facts and interpretations

Session 3: Needs and Values Behind Guilt
  Duration: 25 min
  ✓ Explain that guilt can be a compass pointing to our values
  ✓ Ask: "If there were no guilt, what would you want instead?"
  ✓ Explore needs: safety, connection with others, justice, service
  ✓ Example: guilt about not being at the front → value of service, protection

Session 4: What Was Under My Control?
  Duration: 25 min
  ✓ Explain the difference between guilt (I did something bad) and shame (I am bad)
  ✓ Help honestly assess: what was truly under your control in that situation?
  ✓ Explore external circumstances: war, evacuation, danger, limited resources
  ✓ Ask: "Could you have acted differently, given all the circumstances?"

Session 5: Self-Compassion Practices
  Duration: 30 min
  ✓ Explain that self-compassion is key to healing from guilt
  ✓ Offer a simple meditation: "May I be safe, may I be happy, may I be healthy"
  ✓ Exercise "Letter of compassion to self" — write a letter to yourself with understanding and support
  ✓ Remind that self-compassion doesn't mean abandoning responsibility

Session 6: Reframing and Actions
  Duration: 25 min
  ✓ Return to values identified in Session 3
  ✓ Help find accessible ways to fulfill them:
  ✓ Suggest one small concrete action this week
  ✓ Summarize progress
