🗺️ STEP 5: JOURNEY SESSIONS
─────────────────────────────────────────────────
Session 1: Introduction and Transition Map
  Duration: 25 min
  ✓ Ask about service experience (what was important, what the military provided)
  ✓ Ask how the return is going, what's hardest now
  ✓ Explain that transitioning to civilian life is one of the most difficult transformations
  ✓ Acknowledge that loss of structure, "military family," and purpose is a normal part of the process

Session 2: Identity: Military and Civilian
  Duration: 30 min
  ✓ Explain the concept of social identity and the "military family"
  ✓ Suggest an exercise: "What does military identity give me now? What does it take away?"
  ✓ Discuss cultural differences between military and civilian worlds
  ✓ Help see military skills as a resource, not an obstacle
  ✓ Explain that integrating identities, not abandoning the military one, is key to adaptation

Session 3: Family: How They Understand Us
  Duration: 25 min
  ✓ Acknowledge that family often doesn't understand the incompatibility of military and civilian cultures
  ✓ Teach simple "I-statements" to explain reactions:
  ✓ Explain that veterans and families may speak different languages
  ✓ Suggest ideas for shared activities that bring together
  ✓ Discuss how to involve family in the understanding process (movies, books, conversations)

Session 4: Communicating with Civilians
  Duration: 25 min
  ✓ Explain common unhelpful beliefs about civilians ("they won't understand," "they're weak")
  ✓ Discuss how much these beliefs help adaptation
  ✓ Teach assertive communication (contrasted with passive/aggressive)
  ✓ Practice responses to uncomfortable questions:
  ✓ Explain that most civilians want to support but don't know how

Session 5: Finding New Purpose (Values, Not Obligation)
  Duration: 30 min
  ✓ Explain the difference between values and goals
  ✓ Complete the "Values Circles" exercise — what's important in life (family, service, growth, safety)
  ✓ Discuss how values from service can be realized in civilian life:
  ✓ Suggest options: volunteering, education, business, community activity

Session 6: Friendship and Comrades
  Duration: 20 min
  ✓ Acknowledge that the "military family" is a unique bond, hard to find in civilian life
  ✓ Discuss the importance of maintaining connection with comrades
  ✓ Suggest creating a "social connection map": who's around, who's missing, where to meet people
  ✓ Suggest ways to meet people: veteran spaces, sports, courses, volunteering
  ✓ Explain that new connections don't replace old ones, they complement them

Session 7: Regulation and Flexibility
  Duration: 25 min
  ✓ Explain that military regulation was an adaptive skill that helped survival
  ✓ Discuss where regulation helps in civilian life (discipline, planning)
  ✓ Explore where regulation hinders (relationships, rest, flexibility)
  ✓ Suggest practices for finding balance:
  ✓ Teach relaxation techniques to release excess tension

Session 8: Working with Resentment and Regret
  Duration: 25 min
  ✓ Start with grounding (5-4-3-2-1)
  ✓ Explain the functions of resentment (protection, control) and its impact
  ✓ Suggest reflection: "What's behind my resentment? What's the primary emotion?"
  ✓ Practice self-compassion for one's pain
  ✓ Discuss regret: "What would I have done differently?" → reframing through context ("at that moment, I acted as I could")
  ✓ Help see how resentment and regret can become fuel for new goals

Session 9: Career and Professional Development
  Duration: 25 min
  ✓ Discuss what skills acquired in the military could be valuable in civilian professions
  ✓ Help translate "military" skills into "civilian" language (resume)
  ✓ Share information about retraining programs, veteran grants, business opportunities
  ✓ Suggest exploring fields that align with values
  ✓ Advise reaching out to veteran spaces for consultation

Session 10: Psychological Support and Self-Help
  Duration: 20 min
  ✓ Explain that seeking help is strength, not weakness
  ✓ Discuss barriers: stigma, shame, "I'll handle it myself"
  ✓ Teach recognizing warning signs: sleep disturbances, avoidance, anger outbursts, thoughts of self-harm
  ✓ Create a "Keeping Well Plan":
  ✓ Suggest veteran spaces and support lines
