Effective Use of AI in Work and Daily Life: Evidence Base

This overview serves as the evidence base for the "AI Productivity Coach" role on fromua.life. The information is adapted for Ukrainian users — tool accessibility, language barriers, free alternatives, privacy concerns.

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is no longer a futuristic concept. According to McKinsey, 88% of organizations today use AI in at least one business function, and this trend extends to individual users [1]. However, for most people, the biggest challenge isn't access to tools, but how to use them effectively without deep technical knowledge.

Key Strategies for Effective AI Use

🔹 Proper Prompt Formulation
The quality of AI output directly depends on the quality of the prompt. The more details you provide about purpose, audience, and tone, the better the result [2]. Instead of "Write a letter," write: "Write a short, friendly letter to a client, introducing a new product".
🔹 Using Precise Instructions
Breaking down complex tasks into sequential steps helps AI agents act logically. Defining a role for the AI ("Act as a marketing strategist") and using numbered steps significantly improves response structure [3].
🔹 Requesting Multiple Options
Asking for several options (e.g., 10 email subject lines with different tones) expands creative possibilities and allows you to choose the best one [4].
🔹 Fact-Checking
AI can "hallucinate" — generate fabricated names, statistics, or sources that sound plausible but are not true [5]. It's important to use tools that cite sources (e.g., Perplexity) and verify critical information through authoritative sources.
🔹 Integration into Workflows
Don't try to change everything at once. Start with one task, test the tool, and only then scale up [6].
🔹 Automating Repetitive Tasks
Set up workflows that run automatically: email responses, meeting transcriptions, data analysis [7].
🔹 Choosing the Right Tools
Different tasks require different tools:
🧰 AI Tool Map:
  • Text assistants: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini
  • Image generators: DALL·E, Midjourney, Canva Magic Studio
  • Research tools: Perplexity, NotebookLM
  • Automation: n8n, Zapier
  • Document work: NotebookLM, Claude (PDF analysis)
  • Planning & notes: Notion Q&A
🔹 Learning Through Practice
Treat AI use as a skill that develops over time. Experiment with different formulations, analyze results, and adjust approaches [8].

Ukrainian Context: Tips for Users

Specific Recommendations:

  • Language aspect: most models work best with English, but ChatGPT and Claude understand Ukrainian well. Gemini also supports Ukrainian.
  • Free alternatives: ChatGPT (free version), Claude (free version), Perplexity (free version with limits), Canva (free version with AI features).
  • Privacy: avoid entering confidential data (passwords, document numbers, banking information). For sensitive documents, consider local models or enterprise versions.
  • Internet connection: with unstable connections, some tools (e.g., NotebookLM) allow offline work with pre-loaded materials.
  • Communities: join Ukrainian Telegram channels and Facebook groups dedicated to AI for experience sharing and advice in Ukrainian.

When Professional Help is Needed

Red Flags:
  • You want to use AI to write a thesis/paper without your own input (academic dishonesty)
  • You need help with complex technical API setup
  • You don't understand how to protect your data when working with AI
  • You're afraid AI will replace your job (needs career counseling)
