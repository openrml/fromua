Healthy Escapism as a Recovery Tool: Evidence Base

This overview serves as the evidence base for the "Healthy Escapism Curator" role on fromua.life. The information is adapted for Ukrainians living in wartime conditions — the need to distract from stress, availability of various leisure activities, the risk of excessive escapism as a way to escape reality.

Escapism is the ability to temporarily distract from reality through various activities such as reading, gaming, watching movies, or physical exercise [1]. In scientific literature, escapism often has a negative connotation and is associated with internet addiction, problematic use, and excessive gaming [2]. However, certain aspects of escapism can be beneficial.

Key Findings

🔹 The Four Pillars of Healthy Escapism
Research identifies four key functions of healthy escapism: emotional regulation, mood management, coping with stress, and recovery [3]. Mental distraction through video games helps players reduce stress after intense work, improve mood, and regulate emotions [4].
🔹 The Difference Between Healthy and Harmful Escapism
Healthy escapism helps cope with challenges, manage stress, and safely balance emotions [5]. Harmful escapism leads to loss of time, missed deadlines, social isolation, addictions, and psychological distress. The key question: do you control the activity, or does it control you?

🔹 "Comfort Blanket" Media
Many people have favorite movies, books, or games they return to during difficult times. This isn't "mindless" consumption, but a conscious choice of familiar, safe content that requires no emotional effort [6]. Rewatching old favorite series gives a sense of safety because you know what comes next.
🔹 "Gentle Media"
There's a whole genre of "gentle" content — films, music, books, or games with a calm atmosphere, without sharp sounds, tension spikes, or fear. Such content is never boring but allows you to relax and recover [7].
🔹 Micro-Activities and "Small Wins"
Recovery is often built on small, achievable steps: showing up at an event for 10 minutes, preparing a simple meal, going outside for 5 minutes [8]. These "small wins" create momentum and a sense of control.
🔹 Cozy Games
Games like Animal Crossing, Stardew Valley, and Palia offer a relaxing experience without high stress. They often have themes of care, growing, creating comfort [9]. Such games calm the nervous system and provide a sense of achievement without pressure.
🔹 Physical Activity as Escapism
Using physical activity for a break from real-world stress is a great way to maintain low stress levels and high creativity [10].
🔹 Attention to Motivation
It's important to pay attention to WHY you choose a particular activity. If it's to increase positive emotions and well-being — that's healthy. If it's as punishment or escape from responsibilities — it's worth reconsidering [11].

When Professional Help is Needed

Red Flags:
  • You spend most of the day in games/social media/series and can't stop
  • You've lost a job/relationship because you're constantly "resting"
  • You haven't left the house for weeks because the virtual world is enough
  • You feel like you're running away from life, not just resting
  • You have thoughts of suicide
