  Duration: 40 min
  ✓ Welcome and orientation: explain role, limits, confidentiality
  ✓ Assess immediate needs and safety of the community leader
  ✓ Introduce core principle: "The community is the expert on its own experience"
  ✓ Guide initial community assessment: what are the strengths, needs, key actors?

  ✓ Introduce stakeholder mapping
  Expected Outcomes:

    • Initial community assessment started
    • Stakeholder map begun
    • Leader feels supported and validated

Session 2: Building Trust and Relationships (The Foundation)
  Duration: 40 min
  ✓ Brief check-in
  ✓ Psychoeducation: trust as the currency of community work
  ✓ Discuss strategies for building trust: sustained presence, listening, transparency, follow-through
  ✓ Explore: "What has built trust in this community? What has eroded it?"
  ✓ Practice active listening skills
  ✓ Discuss creating safe spaces for dialogue, especially around difficult topics
  ✓ Plan one trust-building action for the coming week
  Expected Outcomes:
    • Leader understands trust-building as an ongoing practice
    • Active listening skills strengthened
    • Trust-building action planned
    • Leader feels more equipped to cultivate relationships

Session 3: Collaborative Visioning (Co-Creating the Future)
  Duration: 45 min
  ✓ Brief check-in
  ✓ Introduce inclusive visioning methods (World Cafe, participatory planning)
  ✓ Discuss: "Whose voices are usually heard? Whose are missing?"
  ✓ Guide planning an inclusive community visioning process
  ✓ Address challenges: engaging diverse groups, managing conflict, honoring different perspectives
  ✓ Discuss how local history shapes current realities and future possibilities
  ✓ Plan next steps for inclusive visioning
  Expected Outcomes:
    • Leader has framework for inclusive visioning

  Duration: 45 min
