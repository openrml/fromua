🗺️ STEP 5: JOURNEY SESSIONS
─────────────────────────────────────────────────
Session 1: Understanding How Stress Affects Your Relationship
  Duration: 40 min
  ✓ Welcome both partners equally: create safe, balanced space
  ✓ Assess safety: "Is everyone safe? Does anyone need immediate support?"
  ✓ Psychoeducation: how chronic stress and trauma affect couples — support can be mobilized or deteriorated
  ✓ Normalize: "Your struggles are normal reactions to abnormal circumstances"
  ✓ Invite each partner to share their perspective (without interruption)
  ✓ Reflect and validate both experiences
  Expected Outcomes:
    • Couple understands that stress impacts relationships systemically
    • Each partner feels heard and validated
    • Reduced self-blame and blame of partner
    • Foundation for shared work established

Session 2: Safety and Regulation - Finding Your Anchor
  Duration: 45 min
  ✓ Brief grounding and check-in with both partners
  ✓ Introduce concept of safety as foundation for connection
  ✓ Teach individual grounding practices (breathing, feet on floor)
  ✓ Explore: "What helps each of you feel safe? What triggers feelings of unsafety?"
  ✓ Create shared "safety anchors" — things the couple can do together to regulate
  ✓ Discuss how trauma reactions (hyperarousal, numbness) show up in the relationship
  ✓ Emphasize: "You can't connect if you don't feel safe"
  Expected Outcomes:
    • Both partners have individual grounding tools
    • Couple identifies shared safety practices
    • Increased understanding of trauma reactions in the relationship
    • Foundation for deeper connection established

Session 3: Dialogue and Communication - The Language of Feelings
  Duration: 45 min
  ✓ Brief grounding and check-in
  ✓ Introduce concept of "dialogue" as second dimension of hope
  ✓ Teach active listening: one speaks, one reflects back
  ✓ Practice expressing needs without blame using "I statements"
  ✓ Introduce validation: "I hear that you're feeling... That makes sense because..."
  ✓ Discuss how stress shuts down communication and what helps reopen it
  ✓ Practice with a current (low-stakes) topic
  Expected Outcomes:
    • Couple practices active listening and validation
    • Improved ability to express needs without blame
    • Partners feel more "heard" and "understood"
    • Communication skills for ongoing use developed

Session 4: Dyadic Coping - Facing Challenges Together
  Duration: 45 min
  ✓ Brief grounding and check-in
  ✓ Introduce dyadic coping: facing stress as a team, not as opponents
  ✓ Explore current coping patterns: supportive vs negative (withdrawal, criticism)
  ✓ Teach supportive dyadic coping: asking for help, offering support, accepting support
  ✓ Practice: one partner shares a stressor, the other practices supportive response
  ✓ Discuss: "What gets in the way of supporting each other?"
  ✓ Reinforce: "You're on the same team, even when it doesn't feel like it"
  Expected Outcomes:
    • Couple identifies their coping patterns
    • Partners practice supportive dyadic coping
    • Reduced negative coping (criticism, withdrawal)
    • Increased sense of "we're in this together"

Session 5: Rebuilding Intimacy and Connection
  Duration: 45 min
  ✓ Brief grounding and check-in
  ✓ Acknowledge: trauma and stress often impact intimacy (emotional and physical)
  ✓ Explore emotional intimacy: "When do you feel closest to each other?"
  ✓ Discuss physical intimacy: challenges, changes, hopes
  ✓ Introduce shared activities as third dimension of hope
  ✓ Plan one small shared activity for the coming week (not pressure, just connection)
  ✓ Normalize: "Intimacy often needs to be rebuilt slowly, with patience"
  Expected Outcomes:
    • Couple openly discusses intimacy challenges
    • Partners identify ways to reconnect emotionally
    • Shared activity planned
    • Hope for rebuilding connection increased

Session 6: Cognitive Balance and Moving Forward
  Duration: 45 min
  ✓ Brief grounding and check-in
  ✓ Introduce cognitive balance: distinguishing individual thoughts from relational dynamics
  ✓ Explore: "What stories do you each tell about your relationship? Are they the whole story?"
  ✓ Review progress across all sessions
  ✓ Create shared vision for the future (realistic, flexible)
  ✓ Identify "red flags" that require professional help:
  ✓ Plan for continued practice and support
  ✓ Celebrate strength and commitment
  Expected Outcomes:
    • Couple has more balanced view of their relationship
    • Shared future vision developed
    • Red flags identified and action plan known
    • Couple feels more connected and resilient
