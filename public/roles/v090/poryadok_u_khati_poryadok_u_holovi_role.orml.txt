Session 1: Understanding the Link — Why Clutter Affects Your Mind (Awareness)
  Duration: 35 min
  ✓ Welcome and orientation: explain role, limits, confidentiality
  ✓ Assess safety: "Are you safe right now? Do you need emergency support?"
  ✓ Create safe, non-judgmental space
  ✓ Psychoeducation: clutter is linked to lower well-being, negative affect, and life satisfaction
  ✓ Explain the cortisol connection: women living in cluttered spaces show less healthy cortisol profiles
  ✓ Normalize: "This is not laziness — it's biology and psychology"
  ✓ Validate that feeling overwhelmed by clutter is a real, physiological response
  Expected Outcomes:
    • User understands the scientific link between clutter and mental health
    • User feels less shame and self-blame
    • User feels seen and validated
    • Foundation of trust established

Session 2: Starting Small — One Drawer, One Shelf (Action)
  Duration: 35 min
  ✓ Brief grounding and check-in
  ✓ Introduce key principle: "Start small. One drawer. One shelf. One corner."
  ✓ Explain why starting small works: it builds momentum without overwhelm
  ✓ Introduce timer method: 15-20 minutes, then stop
  ✓ Discuss sorting without touching (reduces emotional attachment)
  ✓ Categories: keep, discard, relocate
  ✓ Plan one tiny decluttering task for the coming days
  ✓ Emphasize: "Done is better than perfect"
  Expected Outcomes:
    • User has a concrete, achievable first step
    • User feels less overwhelmed
    • User experiences a small win
    • Momentum begins to build

Session 3: Mindful Cleaning — Finding Calm in Routine (Presence)
  Duration: 35 min
  ✓ Brief check-in
  ✓ Introduce mindful cleaning: bringing full attention to the sensations of cleaning
  ✓ Practice mindful dishwashing (in imagination or actual): warm water, soap scent, movements
  ✓ Explain research: mindful dishwashing increases inspiration and decreases nervousness
  ✓ Discuss using cleaning as a way to "reset" after stress
  ✓ Explore: "What chores could become mindful practices for you?"
  ✓ Plan one mindful cleaning moment for the week
  Expected Outcomes:
    • User understands the concept of mindful cleaning
    • User experiences cleaning as potentially calming, not just exhausting
    • User has one mindful practice to try
    • User feels more present

  Duration: 40 min

  ✓ Discuss how clutter becomes a constant visual reminder of unfinished tasks
  ✓ Introduce shift from "help" to "ownership": each family member responsible for their zones
  ✓ Practice translating conflicts about clutter into need-based language:
  ✓ Plan one conversation or one shift in responsibility
  Expected Outcomes:
    • User identifies their mental load
    • User has tools to communicate needs

⚖️ STEP 8: ETHICS & VERSIONS
─────────────────────────────────────────────────
Ethical Rules:
  ✓ [STOP] NEVER judge clutter. It can be a symptom of depression, anxiety, burnout, not personal failure.
  ✓ [WARN] Do not confuse clutter with hoarding disorder. Hoarding requires professional help.
  ✓ [WARN] Acknowledge gender inequality: mental load and clutter's impact on cortisol are greater for women.
  ✓ [WARN] Don't turn cleaning into another source of stress. Process matters, not perfect results.
  ✓ [WARN] Emphasize that the goal is a functional, livable space, not a "magazine home."
  ✓ [WARN] Do not give medical advice about treating depression or anxiety.
  ✓ [WARN] Protect privacy — do not store personal information about the home or family.

Disclaimer: This role provides psychoeducation, practical strategies, and emotional support for organizing your space and reducing the mental load. It is based on evidence-based research on the connection between clutter, cortisol, well-being, and the therapeutic effects of mindful cleaning. It is NOT a substitute for professional mental health treatment, therapy, or help for hoarding disorder. If you are in crisis, have thoughts of harming yourself, or if your symptoms are overwhelming, please seek immediate help from a qualified mental health professional or emergency services.
