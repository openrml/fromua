🗺️ STEP 5: JOURNEY SESSIONS
─────────────────────────────────────────────────
Session 1: Anchor in the Storm (Acute Relief)
  Duration: 20 min
  ✓ Safety check: "Are you safe right now? Do you need emergency services?"
  ✓ Guide through 4-7-8 breathing (inhale 4, hold 7, exhale 8) to initiate parasympathetic response
  ✓ Lead through 5-4-3-2-1 sensory grounding exercise (5 things you see, 4 you feel, 3 you hear, 2 you smell, 1 you taste)
  ✓ Offer physical grounding (press feet into floor, clench and release fists)
  ✓ Optional: temperature shift technique (splash cold water on face/wrists)
  Expected Outcomes:
    • User reports subjective decrease in panic intensity (from 10/10 to lower)
    • User is oriented to present moment and physical surroundings
    • User learns simple, repeatable techniques for acute distress
    • User feels accompanied and not alone during the episode

Session 2: Understanding Your Alarm System (Psychoeducation)
  Duration: 35 min
  ✓ Explain the "why" behind panic in simple terms (amygdala, false alarm, nervous system)
  ✓ Normalize the experience: "Your brain is trying to protect you, even if it's overreacting"
  ✓ Identify early warning signs and physical sensations that precede panic
  ✓ Explore common triggers (places, situations, thoughts, body sensations)
  ✓ Introduce concept of "fear of fear" cycle
  Expected Outcomes:
    • User understands physiological basis of panic, reducing secondary fear
    • User can identify 3-5 personal early warning signs
    • User recognizes 2-3 common triggers in their life
    • User feels less "broken" or "crazy" about their experience

Session 3: Building Your Grounding Toolkit (CBT Skills)
  Duration: 40 min
  ✓ Review all grounding techniques learned in Session 1
  ✓ Co-create personalized "panic stop plan" listing preferred techniques in order
  ✓ Discuss practice: "These skills work best when practiced when calm"
  ✓ Identify which techniques work best for different situations/severity levels
  ✓ Create a portable "grounding kit" list (items to keep nearby)
  Expected Outcomes:
    • User has written, personalized plan to use during future episodes
    • User identifies 2-3 most effective techniques for their needs
    • User commits to practicing one technique daily for 2 minutes
    • User feels increased sense of control and self-efficacy

Session 4: Unhooking from Panic Thoughts (ACT - Defusion)
  Duration: 40 min
  ✓ Introduce concept of cognitive defusion: thoughts are just thoughts, not facts
  ✓ Practice observing thoughts without engaging ("I notice I'm having the thought that...")
  ✓ Common panic thoughts to practice with: "I'm dying," "I'm losing control," "I'm going crazy"
  ✓ Metaphors: thoughts as passing clouds, radio in the background, leaves on a stream
  ✓ Differentiate between helpful and unhelpful thoughts during panic
  Expected Outcomes:
    • User learns to distance from frightening thoughts during episodes
    • User reports decreased belief in catastrophic thoughts
    • User can practice defusion independently
    • User experiences thoughts as less threatening

Session 5: Values-Based Action (ACT - Commitment)
  Duration: 45 min
  ✓ Explore personal values: What matters to you? What kind of life do you want?
  ✓ Discuss how panic and avoidance have been getting in the way of values
  ✓ Identify small, values-aligned actions to take (even with anxiety present)
  ✓ Create "willingness" statements: "I'm willing to feel anxious in order to..."
  ✓ Plan one small values-based action for the coming week
  Expected Outcomes:
    • User connects their struggle to what deeply matters to them
    • User identifies 1-2 values-based actions to take despite fear
    • User experiences motivation beyond just symptom reduction
    • User begins to shift focus from fighting panic to living fully

Session 6: Relapse Prevention & Resilience
  Duration: 40 min
  ✓ Review progress across all sessions (what's working, what's challenging)
  ✓ Identify early warning signs of relapse or increased frequency
  ✓ Create maintenance plan: daily practices, weekly check-ins
  ✓ Discuss when and how to seek professional help (therapy, medication)
  ✓ Celebrate progress and acknowledge courage
  ✓ Reinforce: "You now have tools for life, not just for panic"
  Expected Outcomes:
    • User has comprehensive long-term mental health maintenance strategy
    • User can identify signs that indicate need for professional support
    • User feels empowered and confident in their coping abilities
    • User understands this is a journey, not a destination
