🗺️ STEP 5: JOURNEY SESSIONS
─────────────────────────────────────────────────
Session 1: Understanding the Distance (Foundation)
  Duration: 40 min
  ✓ Welcome both partners (can be asynchronous or together, depending on availability)
  ✓ Assess safety: "Is everyone safe? Does anyone need immediate support?"
  ✓ Psychoeducation: how war affects communication — the shift to "short, emotionless messages" as survival mode
  ✓ Normalize: "Your struggles are normal reactions to an impossible situation"
  ✓ Invite each partner to share their experience of the separation (can be through messages if synchronous not possible)
  ✓ Validate both experiences equally
  Expected Outcomes:
    • Couple understands that communication difficulties are normal in wartime
    • Each partner feels heard and validated
    • Reduced self-blame and blame of partner
    • Foundation for shared work established

Session 2: The A.R.E. Framework - Accessibility (Being Available)
  Duration: 45 min
  ✓ Brief check-in with both partners
  ✓ Introduce A.R.E. framework: Accessibility, Responsiveness, Engagement
  ✓ Focus on Accessibility: what does it mean to be available to your partner at a distance?
  ✓ Explore current patterns: when do you feel your partner is accessible? When not?
  ✓ Create connection rituals: regular check-ins, "good morning/good night" messages
  ✓ Coordinate communication times that work for both, considering schedules and safety
  ✓ Discuss: "Accessibility isn't about constant contact — it's about reliable connection"
  Expected Outcomes:
    • Couple understands the importance of Accessibility
    • Partners identify ways to be more reliably available
    • Connection rituals established
    • Reduced uncertainty about partner's availability

Session 3: Responsiveness - The Language of Feelings (Being Heard)
  Duration: 45 min
  ✓ Brief check-in
  ✓ Focus on Responsiveness: how do you respond to your partner's emotional needs at a distance?
  ✓ Discuss the challenge: "short, emotionless messages" can feel like rejection — but may be protective
  ✓ Practice empathic responses in different channels:
  ✓ Explore: "What makes you feel your partner truly hears you, even from far away?"
  ✓ Practice validation statements: "I hear that you're feeling... That makes sense because..."
  Expected Outcomes:
    • Improved emotional responsiveness in digital communication
    • Partners feel more heard and understood
    • Reduced misinterpretation of brief messages
    • Tools for expressing care across distance

Session 4: Engagement - Staying Connected (Being Valued)
  Duration: 45 min
  ✓ Brief check-in
  ✓ Focus on Engagement: how do you show your partner they are valued and important?
  ✓ Explore shared activities through technology:
  ✓ Discuss media multiplexity: using different channels for different needs
  ✓ Create a "media menu" — what channel works best for what kind of connection
  Expected Outcomes:
    • Couple identifies shared activities for connection
    • Partners understand media multiplexity and use it intentionally
    • Increased sense of being valued and engaged
    • Richer, more varied communication

Session 5: Navigating Uncertainty (Managing the Unknown)
  Duration: 45 min
  ✓ Brief check-in
  ✓ Psychoeducation: uncertainty is the main challenge in technology-mediated communication
  ✓ Explore sources of uncertainty: delayed responses, limited cues, unknown contexts
  ✓ Discuss what can and cannot be controlled
  ✓ Strategies to reduce uncertainty:
  ✓ Normalize that some uncertainty will always remain — and that's okay
  ✓ Practice: "When I feel uncertain, I can..."
  Expected Outcomes:
    • Couple understands uncertainty as a normal part of distance
    • Partners have strategies to reduce unnecessary uncertainty
    • Reduced anxiety about the unknown
    • Greater tolerance for remaining uncertainty

  ✓ Identify "red flags" that require professional help:
