🗺️ STEP 5: JOURNEY SESSIONS
─────────────────────────────────────────────────
Session 1: Creating Safety and Orientation (Foundation)
  Duration: 35 min
  ✓ Welcome and orientation: explain role, limits, confidentiality
  ✓ Assess safety: "Are you safe right now? Do you need emergency support?"
  ✓ Psychoeducation: trauma and the body — why the body remembers
  ✓ Introduce concept of "window of tolerance"
  ✓ Practice orientation: notice the space around you, where you are, that you are safe now
  ✓ Invite: "Notice what it feels like to be supported by the chair/floor"
  ✓ Emphasize choice: "You can stop anytime"
  Expected Outcomes:
    • User understands that body reactions after trauma are normal
    • User experiences initial sense of safety in the present
    • User learns basic orientation skill
    • User feels in control of their participation

Session 2: Learning the Language of the Body (Interoception)
  Duration: 40 min
  ✓ Brief grounding and check-in
  ✓ Introduce interoception: noticing sensations inside the body
  ✓ Start with neutral or pleasant sensations (warmth, support, breath)
  ✓ Practice gentle body scan from feet to head
  ✓ Notice without judgment: "just noticing, not changing"
  ✓ Identify a "resource" sensation — a place in the body that feels neutral or pleasant
  ✓ Discuss: "The body has its own language. We're learning to listen."
  Expected Outcomes:
    • User can notice basic body sensations
    • User identifies at least one "resource" sensation
    • User experiences increased body awareness without distress
    • User learns to observe rather than react

Session 3: Pendulation and Titration (Gentle Processing)
  Duration: 40 min
  ✓ Brief grounding and connect with resource sensation
  ✓ Introduce pendulation: gently moving between a slight discomfort and the resource
  ✓ Practice: notice a slightly uncomfortable sensation, then return to resource
  ✓ Explain titration: working with small amounts, not overwhelming the system
  ✓ Emphasize: "We only work with what feels manageable"
  ✓ Practice pendulation 2-3 times, always returning to resource
  ✓ End with grounding and resource
  Expected Outcomes:
    • User understands pendulation and titration concepts
    • User can gently explore discomfort without becoming overwhelmed
    • User experiences increased capacity to be with sensations
    • User feels more in control of their experience

Session 4: Grounding and Centering (Finding Stability)
  Duration: 40 min
  ✓ Brief check-in and resource connection
  ✓ Practice grounding: feeling feet on floor, weight in chair, support of earth
  ✓ Introduce centering: finding the physical center of the body (below navel)
  ✓ Practice breath awareness from center
  ✓ Combine grounding and centering: "Rooted and centered"
  ✓ Discuss: "These are anchors you can return to anytime"
  ✓ Encourage daily grounding practice
  Expected Outcomes:
    • User can ground themselves independently
    • User experiences sense of stability and centeredness
    • User has reliable anchors for regulation
    • User feels more present in body

Session 5: Re-establishing Control and Boundaries
  Duration: 40 min
  ✓ Brief grounding and centering
  ✓ Explore sense of personal space: "Notice the space around your body"
  ✓ Practice boundary awareness: hands in lap, sensing edges of body
  ✓ Gentle movement: noticing how it feels to move with choice
  ✓ Distinguish past from present: "This body is here now, not there then"
  ✓ Practice saying "no" internally to any unwanted sensation
  ✓ Reinforce: "You are in charge of your body now"
  Expected Outcomes:
    • User experiences increased sense of control over body
    • User can sense personal boundaries
    • User distinguishes past trauma from present safety
    • User feels empowered in their body

Session 6: Integration and Moving Forward
  Duration: 40 min
  ✓ Review progress across all sessions
  ✓ Identify which practices were most helpful
  ✓ Create personalized "body practice plan" for continued work
  ✓ Identify "red flags" that indicate need for professional help:
  ✓ Practice body gratitude: thank your body for its strength
  ✓ Celebrate courage in reconnecting with the body
  Expected Outcomes:
    • User has personalized plan for continued somatic practice
    • User can identify when professional help is needed
    • User experiences appreciation for their body
    • User feels more at home in their body
