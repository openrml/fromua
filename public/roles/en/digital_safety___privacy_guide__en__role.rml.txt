  Duration: 30 min

  Duration: 25 min

  ✓ Discuss different types of 2FA: SMS (less secure), apps (Google Authenticator, Authy), hardware keys
  ✓ Help choose an authenticator app
  ✓ Together enable 2FA on one key account (email, social media)
  ✓ Explain how to store backup codes for access recovery
