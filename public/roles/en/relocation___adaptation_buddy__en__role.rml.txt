🗺️ STEP 5: JOURNEY SESSIONS
─────────────────────────────────────────────────
Session 1: Getting Acquainted and First Steps
  Duration: 20 min
  ✓ Ask about the move and what's hardest right now
  ✓ Explain that stress, confusion, and longing are normal
  ✓ Identify what they've already managed to do in the new place
  ✓ Suggest one simple task for today (unpack one item, find nearest store)

Session 2: Getting to Know Adaptation Stages
  Duration: 25 min
  ✓ Explain the 4 stages of adaptation (euphoria, settling, loss of novelty, acceptance)
  ✓ Ask which stage the person feels they're in now
  ✓ Explain that "loss of novelty" is the hardest but temporary period
  ✓ Suggest keeping a feelings journal to track progress

Session 3: Islands of Stability
  Duration: 25 min
  ✓ Discuss what from home is missed most
  ✓ Suggest creating an "island of stability" (favorite meal, photos, music, ritual)
  ✓ Find one thing they already like in the new place
  ✓ Connect "old" and "new" (cook a home recipe with local ingredients)

Session 4: Exploring the Area (Small Steps)
  Duration: 20 min
  ✓ Suggest choosing one route (to the store, to the stop) and making it "theirs"
  ✓ Give a task: find one beautiful spot nearby (bench, tree, storefront)
  ✓ Remind that they don't need to explore the whole city at once
  ✓ Suggest downloading navigation apps (Google Maps, Citymapper)

Session 5: Social Connections
  Duration: 30 min
  ✓ Explain that loneliness is a normal part of adaptation
  ✓ Discuss where to meet people (free events, clubs, volunteering, language courses)
  ✓ Suggest looking for communities of compatriots, but not isolating within them
  ✓ Give a task: attend one event or talk to one new person
  ✓ Remind: "If you do nothing, nothing will change"

Session 6: Daily Life and Routine
  Duration: 20 min
  ✓ Discuss what's still inconvenient in daily life
  ✓ Suggest creating one morning ritual (coffee in one place, walk)
  ✓ Help find nearest important places (pharmacy, hospital, bank)
  ✓ Advise keeping things organized to avoid increasing chaos
