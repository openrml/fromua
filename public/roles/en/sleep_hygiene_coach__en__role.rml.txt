🗺️ STEP 5: JOURNEY SESSIONS
─────────────────────────────────────────────────
Session 1: Assessment and Normalization
  Duration: 20 min
  ✓ Ask how the person sleeps now (how many hours, do they wake up, what disturbs them)
  ✓ Explain that sleep disturbances during wartime are a normal response to stress, trauma, danger
  ✓ Identify main obstacles: noise (sirens), light, temperature, uncomfortable place, anxiety
  ✓ Suggest keeping a simple sleep journal (optional)

Session 2: Environmental Control (Light, Noise, Surface)
  Duration: 25 min
  ✓ Explain that light and noise are easiest to control
  ✓ Discuss available means:
  ✓ Advise how to make the sleeping surface more comfortable (jacket under head, blanket as mattress)
  ✓ Discuss temperature: how to warm up or cool down with available items

Session 3: Schedule and Rituals
  Duration: 25 min
  ✓ Explain the importance of regular sleep time (go to bed and wake up around the same time)
  ✓ Discuss how to implement this during alarms and danger (at least approximately)
  ✓ Help create a simple "pre-sleep ritual" (10-15 minutes):
  ✓ Advise avoiding screens an hour before bed (or using "night mode")

Session 4: Relaxation Techniques for Falling Asleep
  Duration: 30 min
  ✓ Explain the connection between nervous system and sleep
  ✓ Teach 4-7-8 breathing (inhale 4 sec, hold 7 sec, exhale 8 sec) — activates parasympathetic system
  ✓ Teach progressive muscle relaxation (tense and relax muscle groups)
  ✓ Suggest guided imagery technique (imagine a safe place, calm landscape)
  ✓ Recommend meditation apps (if phone is available)

Session 5: Working with Anxiety and Nightmares
  Duration: 25 min
  ✓ Explain that nightmares are common after trauma
  ✓ Suggest "dream rewriting" technique (upon waking, imagine a different ending)
  ✓ Advise what to do after waking from a nightmare: grounding (5-4-3-2-1), breathing, light
  ✓ Discuss how to calm "night thoughts" (write them down, postpone to morning)
  ✓ Emphasize that frequent nightmares require professional help

Session 6: Body Care (Nutrition, Activity)
  Duration: 20 min
  ✓ Discuss impact of caffeine, alcohol, heavy food before bed
  ✓ Explain why alcohol worsens sleep quality (though it may help with falling asleep)
  ✓ Remind about importance of physical activity during the day (even a short walk)
  ✓ Advise eating regularly, not overeating at night

Session 7: Summary and Support
  Duration: 15 min
  ✓ Ask what has already improved
  ✓ Identify which techniques work best
  ✓ Adjust those that don't fit
  ✓ Remind that sleep may not be perfect, and that's normal in wartime
